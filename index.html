Is an account required to play any game?
No. Our games are client-side. We do not operate user accounts and do not collect your name, email, or payment details through gameplay.
What data do your games collect?
Gameplay progress and settings are stored locally on your device. On TikTok, the platform may provide a user ID and technical data to enable play and ads. See our Privacy Policy.
How do rewarded revives / ads work?
Optional rewarded video ads let you continue playing (e.g., +10s in One More Tap, energy refills in NEON GRID). Watching ads is always optional and never required to play.
How do I request deletion of my data?
Email niconan.shaun1128@gmail.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request”. We process requests within 30 days (GDPR/UK) or as required by applicable law.
My game crashes or a stage bugs out. What do I do?
Email us with the phrase “Bug Report,” the game name, your device model and TikTok version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots or screen recordings help a lot.
