Is an account required to play?
No. One More Tap is a client-side game. We do not operate user accounts and do not collect your name, email, or payment details through the game.
What data does the game collect?
Gameplay progress (scores and settings) is stored locally on your device. When played inside TikTok, the TikTok platform may provide a user ID and device/technical data to enable play and ads. See our Privacy Policy.
How do rewarded revives work?
When you fail a stage, you may choose to watch an optional rewarded video ad to resurrect at that stage and receive +10 seconds on your timer bank. Watching ads is always optional.
How do I request deletion of my data?
Email niconan.shaun1128@gmail.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request”. We will process your request and confirm within 30 days (GDPR/UK) or as required by applicable law.
My game crashes or a stage bugs out. What do I do?
Email us with the phrase “Bug Report,” your device model and TikTok version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots or screen recordings help a lot.
