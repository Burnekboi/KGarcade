1. Introduction

Welcome to One More Tap (“the Game,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how it is protected when you play One More Tap on the TikTok Mini Games platform or through our web build.

By launching, playing, or accessing the Game, you acknowledge and agree to the collection and use of information described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

One More Tap is designed as a client-side hyper-casual game. We do not operate external user account databases and do not collect personally identifiable information (such as your legal name, physical address, phone number, or email address) through gameplay.

The Game stores the following gameplay metrics locally on your device:

  • High scores for Weird Mode and Crazy Mode
  • Cumulative run counts and total taps
  • Highest combo streaks
  • Unlocked achievements
  • Audio preferences (mute / sound enabled)

This data remains on your device so your progress is preserved between sessions. You can clear it at any time by clearing the Game’s local storage or reinstalling the Game.

B. TikTok Mini Game Platform & User ID

When you play One More Tap inside the TikTok application, the TikTok / ByteDance platform may automatically provide a TikTok user identifier (user ID) along with routine technical information such as device model, operating system version, network connection, and region. This is required for the platform to operate the mini game and to validate rewarded-ad rewards.

  • One More Tap does not receive or store your private TikTok account credentials or password.
  • We use the platform-provided user ID only to track gameplay session state and to attribute rewarded-ad rewards to your session.
  • Processing of this platform data is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.

C. Advertisements

The Game integrates TikTok’s rewarded video ads. Ads are served by TikTok / ByteDance, which may process device and usage data independently under its own privacy policy. We do not receive the ad’s targeting details, and we never sell data collected through the Game.

3. How We Use Information

We use information collected for the following purposes:

  • To run the Game and deliver the gameplay experience you request.
  • To persist your progress and preferences across sessions.
  • To serve and validate optional rewarded videos and grant rewards.
  • To maintain game integrity, prevent cheating, and debug technical issues.
  • To respond to support requests you send us by email.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use your data for profiling, targeted advertising beyond the platform’s ad system, or any purpose outside what is described here.

4. Advertising

One More Tap is free to play and distributes optional rewarded video ads. Watching commercials is never mandatory to play the Game. Rewarded ads are provided and mediated by the TikTok / ByteDance platform under its own terms and privacy policy. To learn about their data practices, visit the TikTok Privacy Policy.

5. Data Storage & Retention

Gameplay progress is stored only on your device. Platform-provided identifiers and technical data are stored and retained by the TikTok / ByteDance platform in accordance with their retention practices. On our side, we retain email correspondence (including your email address) only as long as needed to resolve your request.

6. Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data only with:

  • Service providers who help operate the Game and its infrastructure.
  • Platform partners such as TikTok / ByteDance, operating their services under their own policies.
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of players and the public.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: request a copy of personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure (right to be forgotten): request deletion of your data.
  • Restriction and objection: object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Data portability (GDPR): receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Know, delete, and non-discrimination (CCPA): know what we collect, request deletion, and not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw any consent you previously gave at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email niconan.shaun1128@gmail.com with the subject line “Data Deletion Request” (or the relevant right). We will respond within 30 days as required by GDPR / UK GDPR, or as required by applicable law. You may also file a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age set by applicable law in your region) and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page, and the “Last Updated” date at the top reflects the latest revision. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
